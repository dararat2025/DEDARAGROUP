About DE’DARA Group

DE’DARA Group brings together Thai culture, learning and wellbeing, with a connection between Thailand and Jersey.

Our Story

Founded by Dara, DE’DARA Group draws on her Thai roots, personal journey and life experiences. At its heart is a desire to share knowledge, encourage personal growth and bring people together.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create welcoming opportunities for children and adults to explore Thai language, cookery, Muay Thai and cultural traditions through classes, events and shared experiences.

Our Book

DE’DARA: From Country Girl to Ultrarich Lifestyle is a self-help book based on Dara’s true story and life experiences, sharing personal lessons and reflections.

Our Values

Kindness, respect, honesty and cultural understanding guide the experiences we aim to create.
